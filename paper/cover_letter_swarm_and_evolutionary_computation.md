# Cover Letter: Swarm and Evolutionary Computation

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, "Labyrinth Breach: A Reproducible Unity
ML-Agents Benchmark for Asymmetric Multi-Agent Pursuit-Evasion in Dynamic
Mazes," for publication in Swarm and Evolutionary Computation.

The manuscript contributes a reproducible simulation benchmark and empirical
study for asymmetric multi-agent pursuit-evasion. Three cooperative Sentinel
agents and two Runner agents interact in dynamic mazes with exit objectives,
partial observability, tactical reward shaping, and asymmetric self-play. The
study reports five independently trained policy seeds, 30 canonical evaluation
cells, 3,002 completed episodes, lightweight control policies, and two paired
deployment interventions.

The paper fits the journal through its focus on swarm-like coordination,
multi-agent learning, self-play, benchmark design, reproducible evaluation, and
empirical analysis of collective behavior under adversarial pressure. We frame
the contribution conservatively as a benchmark and audited simulation study,
not as a new MARL algorithm or hardware robotics claim.

The submission includes code and model availability, data availability,
author-contribution information, conflict declaration, and a reproducibility
path from raw logs to tables and figures.

Sincerely,

Ahmed Jawed
