# Cover Letter: Robotics and Autonomous Systems

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, "Labyrinth Breach: A Reproducible Unity
ML-Agents Benchmark for Asymmetric Multi-Agent Pursuit-Evasion in Dynamic
Mazes," for publication in Robotics and Autonomous Systems.

The manuscript presents a configurable robotics-simulation benchmark for
multi-agent pursuit-evasion under dynamic topology and partial observability.
The environment combines asymmetric teams, continuous control, maze navigation,
exit-directed evasion, shifting walls, event-level reward provenance, and a
strict artifact audit. The study reports five independent curriculum-trained
policy seeds, 30 canonical assist-off evaluation cells, 3,002 completed
episodes, control-policy baselines, and paired interventions for action
assistance and dynamic walls.

The paper is aligned with autonomous-systems research because it studies
navigation, sensing limits, adversarial interaction, multi-agent coordination,
and reproducible simulation evaluation. The claims are bounded to simulation
and benchmark evidence, with no unsupported hardware-transfer claim.

The submission includes code and model availability, data availability,
author-contribution information, conflict declaration, and a reproducibility
path from raw logs to reported figures and tables.

Sincerely,

Ahmed Jawed
